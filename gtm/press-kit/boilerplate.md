## 200 words

AgentGuardian is an open-source (Apache-2.0) red-teaming toolkit for
AI agents — the LangGraph, CrewAI, MCP, RAG, and REST-API agents
entering production in 2024–2026. You point it at a target endpoint
and it deploys a swarm of 14 specialist adversarial agents under a
Swarm Commander LLM that performs convergence detection. Empirically,
the swarm finds 2.3x more unique finding-classes than serial single-
prompt scanners in the same wall-clock budget.

Every finding is triple-tagged with OWASP ASI 2026, MITRE ATLAS
v5.4.0, and the CSA Agentic AI Red Teaming Guide, and assigned a
deterministic 0–100 AIVSS score (formula open, ablation results
public). Output is SARIF — uploads cleanly into GitHub Code Scanning
— plus JSON, JUnit, Markdown, and PDF. Exit codes are gate-friendly.

AgentGuardian is built by Glacien Technologies, a bootstrapped
security-research company. The toolkit runs entirely on your machine:
no telemetry, no signup, no cloud dependency. A live testbench at
agent-guardian-testbench-u6tm6gzysq-uc.a.run.app demonstrates five
deliberately vulnerable agents you can scan in your browser, no
install. Apache-2.0. Code at
github.com/glacien-technologies/agent-guardian.
