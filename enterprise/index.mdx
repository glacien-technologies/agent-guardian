AgentGuardian (the open-source toolkit) is a free, Apache-2.0 licensed, local-first
red-teaming toolkit for AI agents. It does everything documented on
this site forever, without ever requiring a paid upgrade.

**AgentGuardian Enterprise** is a separate commercial product from
Glacien (the company behind the open-source project). It is built on
the same scanner and probe corpus, plus the layers that don't belong
in OSS:

- Team workflows (SSO, RBAC, multi-user dashboards).
- Managed evidence packs (centralised, retention-controlled storage).
- Runtime controls (live policy enforcement, request gating).
- Audit dashboards (SOC-2 / ISO-27001 / NIST AI RMF reporting views).
- Policy governance (per-org allowlists, change-control workflows).
- Commercial support (named contact, SLA, prioritised bug fixes).

Learn more at [glacien.ai](https://glacien.ai) or contact
[sales@glacien.ai](mailto:sales@glacien.ai).

<Info>
The OSS docs deliberately do not include a feature comparison, pricing,
or sales copy. AgentGuardian (the open-source toolkit) is fully usable for serious
red-teaming without ever touching the Enterprise tier. See
[Open vs Enterprise](/concepts/open-vs-enterprise) for the conceptual
boundary.
</Info>
